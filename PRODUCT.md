## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Commerce operators open the SourceMedium foundation dashboard in their own Metabase to see how sales, spend, products and customer retention changed. A marketer follows the platform and campaign breakdowns. An analyst can open the underlying question or change the installed copy.

The combined dashboard has a tab per business question. Each view is also available as a standalone dashboard. Only views with available source tables appear. Use one reporting currency when comparing money. Date and comparison filters, the reporting store, and the cohort cutoff establish the meaning of a number.

## Review priorities

Read the headline measures before their breakdowns. Show the comparison a change refers to. Distinguish order-based revenue from platform-reported revenue. Keep the field dictionary and metric definitions available in the Data view.
