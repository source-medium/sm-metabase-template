# Foundation dashboard design

Use Metabase's native cards, filters, tabs and dashboard grid. The foundation owns question titles, display settings, column labels, chart series, card order and card dimensions. Metabase supplies navigation, themes and the responsive grid.

- A number card leads with its measure. A comparison belongs beside that measure. A rise in ad spend alone has no good or bad direction.
- Distinguish the comparison series from the selected period. Show chart units and use readable names for series and columns.
- Keep related measures together. At phone width the stacked reading order should still lead from summary to detail.
- Allow a wide table to scroll inside its card. Its columns must not widen the whole dashboard.
- Money has two decimals and no currency symbol. Keep counts, ratios and percentages in the units the warehouse publishes.
- Scope filters to the cards that read them. Keep shared filters in the dashboard bar and place a section-specific control with its section.
