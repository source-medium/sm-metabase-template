# Getting help

- **Your SourceMedium data, Dashboards & Apps or key files:** open a support
  request from your SourceMedium workspace
  (https://sourcemedium.com/docs/product-tours/get-support) or email
  support@sourcemedium.com.
- **A bug in this template:** open an issue with your Metabase version, the
  command you ran (without the token path's contents) and the plan's counts.
  Never paste a key, token or plan file into an issue.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

SourceMedium maintains this template, not the changes in your copy or your
Metabase instance.
